# SECURITY PRACTICAL VOICE TRAINER — 2026-08-30

## Final status

**CLOSED — do not build, publish an offer, or spend on App Store development.**

Candidate ID: `SECURITY_PRACTICAL_VOICE_TRAINER`

Exact workflow considered:

`交通誘導警備業務2級などの受験者が実技・口述手順を選ぶ → iPhoneへ話す → 端末内音声認識で必須語・順序・時間を採点 → 抜けた手順の反復と模擬口述を受け取る → 買切りアプリ`

This was a `RESEARCH_ONLY` lead. No product, offer, payment page or product code was built.

## What was verified

### Paid preparation market exists

Current spending evidence includes:
- paid academic iOS app: JPY 800, public ratings/reviews
- free/freemium academic apps with 300–1,200 questions
- official/special-course route: JPY 33,000 for current guards
- preliminary course: JPY 6,600 at the Special Security Guard Training Center
- general six-day course: JPY 79,200
- textbooks, association e-learning and practical/video substitutes

This proves spending on exam preparation. It does not prove demand for voice-scored solo practical training.

### The exact annual market is narrow

A 2026 trade report citing the Special Security Guard Training Center states that in FY2025:
- all special courses: 14,262 attendees, 10,478 passes
- traffic-guidance level 2: 129 courses, **6,489 attendees**, 4,266 passes
- pass rate: 65.7%

The traffic-guidance level-2 cohort is the most relevant initial market. Industry size (about 588,000 guards) is not the app's addressable annual buyer count; the annual exam-prep cohort is the relevant denominator.

### App economics fail the target

Hypothetical one-time price: JPY 2,980.

Apple's Small Business Program commission is 15%; Apple Developer Program membership is USD 99/year.

To leave JPY 2.4M/year after only a 15% store commission, before tax, refunds, content review, support, promotion and the annual developer fee:

`2,400,000 / (2,980 × 0.85) = 947.5`

Required sales:
- **948 sales/year**
- about **79 sales/month**
- **14.6% of the entire FY2025 traffic-guidance level-2 special-course cohort**

The previous 816-sales estimate was only gross-sales arithmetic. At 816 sales, proceeds after a 15% commission would be about JPY 2,066,928 before all other costs and tax, below the stated take-home goal.

A new app with no owned audience cannot reasonably assume 14.6% national penetration of the full annual cohort. Expanding to more security certifications would multiply proprietary content, review and update burden and would no longer be the same validated workflow.

## Demand evidence failed the gate

Searches included Japanese and English combinations of:
- traffic-guidance level-2 practical/oral practice
- solo practice / no practice partner
- speech scoring / voice app
- mock practical examination
- App Store and Google Play
- paid course, textbook and DVD substitutes

Results found:
- multiple academic-question apps
- app reviews complaining that questions were too easy or unlike the real wording
- company/association articles saying practical preparation is difficult and commonly supported by instructors or coworkers
- training stories involving repeated vocal practice

Results did **not** produce ten independent, repeated public complaints from target buyers specifically asking for a solo, speech-scored practical/oral trainer. The strongest evidence was provider/company commentary rather than direct purchase intent.

Therefore the candidate fails its own kill criterion:

`Fewer than 10 repeated public complaints about solo practical/oral practice.`

## Content accuracy and rights fail the safety gate

Risks:
- practical test content changed in 2026; an industry association held a dedicated briefing because part of the traffic-guidance level-2 practical exam changed
- direct-examination public notices often disclose detailed practical instructions only after written-test qualification
- current learning materials, manuals and practical videos are sold or delivered through industry associations and training bodies
- speech recognition can check words/order/time but cannot validate flag, baton, positioning, movement, visibility, timing against vehicles or other physical performance
- a false “pass-ready” score could cause harm to users and would require qualified, current content review

No legally usable, current, complete practical/oral rubric was secured. The project owner has security-work experience but no recorded examiner/instructor qualification that would solve this content-authority problem.

Therefore the candidate fails additional kill criteria:
- no legally usable and current practical/oral rubric
- qualified review creates material fixed/update work
- voice-only scoring cannot validate the complete practical examination outcome

## Competition conclusion

No exact security-practical speech-scoring app was confirmed. That is not sufficient to continue.

Adjacent technical workflow is already common in:
- interview rehearsal apps
- speech-coaching apps
- pronunciation and presentation scoring

The technical implementation is not a moat. The moat would have to be current, authoritative, legally usable practical content plus trusted distribution. Neither was established.

## Acquisition conclusion

App Store listings prove that academic exam-prep searches exist. They do not prove:
- search volume for practical/oral preparation
- attainable ranking
- a CAC compatible with a one-time JPY 2,980 price
- 79 sales every month

There is no owned channel with measured traffic from security-certification candidates. The existing YouTube audience is not this buyer segment and its previous automated finance tactic is paused.

## Decision

Close the candidate now rather than move it to `OFFER_TEST`.

Do not:
- build a prototype
- copy or reconstruct proprietary practical materials
- publish a preorder page
- pay Apple developer fees for this candidate
- broaden to other examinations to rescue the economics without a new full gate
